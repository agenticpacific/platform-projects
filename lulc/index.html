Evolving Land Use and Land Cover Product for Fiji

Monthly, Quarterly and Annual Cadence from 2016 onwards. Generated using open Foundation Models for Earth Observations, Satellite Embeddings and other GeoAI methodologies. This is a continuously evolving product, with the 2026 layers updated at every Sentinel 2 acquisition with less than 30% cloud cover threshold. Gaps are due to of insufficient Sentinel 2 tiles, or cloud cover exceeding threshold, during that particular time-frame. Contact info@agenticpacific.com.fj for derived-data and analytical products.
